Zhao et al. A publicly available dataset of continuous glucose monitoring records
in patients with diabetes. Scientific Data 10, 35 (2023).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-01940-7

Source archive: Figshare diabetes_datasets.zip, article 21600933, version 5.
https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/diabetes_datasets_zip/21600933

Changes made by GlucoTwin: matched T2DM recording IDs to historical profiles;
retained only timestamped measured CGM observations and an allowlist of baseline
context; removed invalid/missing glucose and duplicate timestamps; normalized
field names and serialized to CSV/JSON. No imputation or resampling is performed.
See processed/manifest.json for the exact checksum, counts and exclusion audit.

The source data are research records. GlucoTwin predictions have not undergone
prospective clinical validation and must not be used for treatment decisions.
